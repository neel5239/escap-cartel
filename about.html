Our story

You start as strangers.
You leave as a cartel.

Escape Cartel isn't a travel agency. It's the friend group you didn't have yet — built one trip at a time.

CHAPTER ONE

Every college has the same story.

Classes, assignments, the same three canteens, the same group chat that talks about "a trip" for months and never books one. You want to get off campus and actually see something — but plans die the second someone's schedule doesn't line up.

Budget hostels feel unsafe to figure out alone. Big travel agencies feel like a school excursion. And the friend group you'd actually want to go with? Half of them haven't even met yet.

We built Escape Cartel because "we should travel together" kept dying in the group chat — and someone finally had to plan it.

CHAPTER TWO

The first sunrise.

It started with a gap year — one person, solo, across India on buses, trains and whatever came next. Somewhere in that year, stuck without a plan more than once, the idea formed: what if the planning was done, and all you had to do was show up?

Then college happened. The first team didn't last. So instead of building an app nobody had asked for, we did something smaller and scarier: planned one sunrise trek in the Aravalis and asked people to trust us with it.

Three registrations in three hours. Then silence. Then it flipped — 50 registrations in 4 hours, sold out in a day. 50 strangers showed up at 4:30 AM, and that trek became Escape Cartel's real birthday.

CHAPTER THREE

Not a tour. Not a package.

A bunch of strangers who become your people. That's the whole idea. Escape Cartel is a crew-led, human-first travel community — every trip is intentionally crafted to connect people, not just move them from point A to point B.
