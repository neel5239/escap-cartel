The fine print, minus the fine

Policies.

Short, honest, and written by humans. Questions? WhatsApp us.

Cancellation & refunds

All trips are non-refundable. Buses, stays and permits are booked for a fixed headcount the day registrations open — that money leaves our account before yours arrives.

  • Can't make it? Swap your seat to a friend for free. Tell us on WhatsApp with their name and number, any time before departure.
  • If Escape Cartel cancels or reschedules a trip, you get 100% back, no questions, within 5 working days.
  • Weather or government-ordered cancellations mid-trip: we refund unused, recoverable costs.

Payments

We accept UPI only. Your seat is reserved for 24 hours after you submit a UTR; it's confirmed once we verify the payment (usually within a few hours). Unverified bookings are released after 24 hours. There are no convenience fees — the price you see is the price you pay.

Safety & conduct

Every trip has a verified host and a certified lead on treks. Alcohol and substances are not allowed on treks or in vehicles. We reserve the right to send anyone home, at their cost, for behaviour that endangers the group. Consent and respect are non-negotiable — report anything to the host or on WhatsApp, and it will be handled immediately.

Privacy

We collect your name, phone, email and college to run trips and contact you about them. We never sell your data. Payment screenshots are used only to verify your booking. Trip photos may appear on our socials — tell your host if you'd rather not be featured, and we'll leave you out.

Reviews

Only travellers with a completed, verified booking can post a review. We don't pay for reviews and we don't delete honest negative ones.
